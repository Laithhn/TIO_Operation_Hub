# Business process gaps and operational governance risks

**Audit date:** 10 November 2026 (Asia/Dubai). **Target:** https://nb01994.vitronic.de/. **Observed account:** TIO Manager. This assessment uses the live snapshots captured during the audit and a separately labelled read-only source review. It does not treat the existence of a button, endpoint or source file as a passed workflow.

The application provides useful operational registers and management views, but the recorded backlog, missing accountability and incomplete service evidence need management attention. Those conditions are **observed data/governance risks**, not proof of software failure. Formal change/release controls and some operational capabilities were not demonstrated in the inspected application; these are **potential scope gaps requiring owner decisions**, not confirmed defects or newly invented requirements.

The live footer identifies **2.59.0**, with a Project dashboard. The local source inventory identifies **2.60.0**, where the latest decision moves recorded status to clients. Consequently, source-only capability statements are not evidence of what is deployed. [Live Management dashboard](traces/011-management-settled.txt), [source inventory](traces/source-inventory.md).

## Interpretation and workflow clarity

- **Separate software defects from operational conditions.** The client Tickets area displaying an internal status differently from the ticket register is a reported software consistency defect; it is handled in the [detailed defect report](04-detailed-bug-report.md). The negative identifier displayed in Jira mapping change history is also handled there; actual business foreign-key corruption or a broken record link was not demonstrated. Neither converts the whole backlog or missing-data population into a software defect. [Client operational snapshot](traces/044-client-operational.txt), [ticket register](traces/016-tickets-settled.txt), [source explanation](traces/source-inventory.md), [audit mapping analysis](traces/audit-mapping-source-analysis.md).
- **Distinguish accountable owner from Jira assignee.** Client summaries use the hub owner; the ticket register can display a Jira assignee/client contact as a fallback. Clarify labels and decision rules before using Unassigned as a single proxy for absent human responsibility. The source demonstrates different display semantics, not an incorrect ownership write. [Source explanation](traces/source-inventory.md).
- **Respect reporting populations and windows.** Executive current workload, selected-period trends and My work are different scopes. The executive upcoming-commitment window of 14 days has documented intent, while a configurable 7-day value feeds other views. Clarifying setting scope or approving one shared window is an enhancement; differing explicit windows alone do not prove a defect. [Window analysis](traces/dashboard-window-and-holiday-analysis.md).
- **Keep absence, unknown and zero distinct.** No recorded project status, no environment observation, no applicable SLA target and no active escalation mean different things. In the inspected sample, the application exposes Unknown/Not available and data-trust disclosures; management should preserve those distinctions instead of reading an empty or zero result as assurance. [Management view](traces/011-management-settled.txt), [SLA view](traces/010-sla-settled.txt), [Project view](traces/012-project-settled.txt).

## Management action sequence

1. **Now:** assign service leads to the Critical/High unassigned queue; validate the oldest overdue records; reconcile the two Jira mapping exceptions and CT-1001 holiday semantics through approved owners. These are governance actions, not authorization for the auditor to change production data.
2. **Next operational review:** prioritize production ownership/version/specification gaps, current environment evidence, recorded project status and the four overdue payment records. Agree measurable exception handling and review cadence.
3. **Before relying on automated compliance or formal go-live approval:** decide the systems of record, supported contract calendar, independent approval obligations and required readiness/change/release controls. Verify a representative workflow with approved test accounts/data.
4. **Before enterprise assurance:** run the blocked mutation, role-isolation, export, scheduled-report and continuity scenarios in an approved isolated environment. Use those results with the confirmed software findings for the release decision; this business-process assessment alone does not establish enterprise readiness.

No implementation effort estimates are assigned: requirements, operating model, dependencies and approved test environments remain necessary inputs. No data, rights, configuration, integration mapping, business record, database or seed change was performed in preparing this report.
