## G. Recommended Hub improvements

These are recommendations based on observed current workflows, not implemented software changes. Existing rights/configuration patterns should be reused, with server-side enforcement and idempotent upgrade defaults for any future implementation.

| Rank | Improvement | Observed reason | Concrete behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Shared client/project activity journal | Private notes cannot serve as a team-wide operational record. Add shared visibility with explicit rights and preserve private notebooks. | Publish approved summaries with source links; never change existing note sharing automatically. |
| 2 | Daily review queue connected to Outlook | Show incoming/sent conversations beside existing records, unresolved actions and previous review cutoff. | Client-by-client review state, alias mapping, thread deduplication, excluded-date evidence and a persisted review ledger. |
| 3 | Progress on commitments and explicit dependencies | Tickets have Record Progress; commitments currently expose completion/deadline controls without equivalent progress. | Add evidence, blocker, next action/owner, next review and progress history while preserving original deadline; link dependencies across records. |
| 4 | Evidence-based status and approval gates | A quote, reported resolution, invitation or proposed VM can be mistaken for completion. | Separate requested/proposed, approved, implemented, validated, accepted; flag missing evidence and conflicting project/SLA states. |
| 5 | Commercial opportunity pipeline | Commercial intelligence is currently held in client notes rather than structured opportunity stages. | Track opportunity, stage, value/currency/basis, owner, next action, decision date and project conversion; separate supplier costs, fee waivers, historical billing and revenue. |
| 6 | Imported-ticket triage and synchronization visibility | Many imported next actions remain generic; local work must respect Jira-controlled status/owners. | Prioritize missing owners/overdue reviews/customer blockers; show source timestamp, field authority and meaningful deltas; avoid duplicate case creation. |
| 7 | Evidence/attachment reconciliation tools | DR38rules/18 TBC endpoints, invoice rows, sizing discrepancies and period labels require manual checks. | Version source attachments; capture page/sheet/range, readability status, calculated totals and discrepancies; keep unverified fields explicit. |
| 8 | Reliable autosave and verification | One note body initially failed to persist while the title saved. | Visible saving/saved/error states, debounce-aware navigation protection, conflict detection, retry and version history with reopen verification. |
| 9 | Operational dependency and expiry alerts | Licence activation, API expiry, missing IPs and procurement block daily delivery. | Track owner, expiry, dependent service, evidence and escalation; store secret references only, never credential values. |
| 10 | Management reporting and review quality metrics | Current snapshots do not establish reliable trends or proof of complete review. | Generate client coverage, changed records, manual-review gaps, unowned actions and evidence freshness from a review ledger; separate review dates from delivery deadlines. |

Suggested implementation sequence: first shared journal plus a persisted daily-review queue; then commitment progress/dependencies and status gates; then opportunity pipeline, attachment reconciliation and management analytics. Include autosave reliability in the first phase. Add acceptance checks for source evidence, correct relationships, access rights and recovery from failed saves. Do not automatically publish existing private notes or automatically approve source-email requests.
