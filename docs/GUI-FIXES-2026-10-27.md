Confirmed on the development machine: `netsh http show urlacl` lists `http://+:80/Reports/` for `NT SERVICE\ReportServer`. | The Reporting Center is at `/reporting-center`; `/reports` redirects inside the hub (query kept). checks that `/clients` loads neither the map nor the geometry. The remaining entry chunk is mostly react-dom (~640 kB unminified), the
framework's complete icon set (`icons.json`, ~265 kB unminified, imported by the vendored framework) and react-router; reducing those needs a
framework change. Load timings (LCP/INP) under throttling were **not** measured: no Lighthouse is installed and no network profile was agreed.

## Open points

- **GUI-01** depends on the server: the fix avoids the SSRS reservation by path. Verified on the development machine (reservation present; hub served on its own port). Not verified on an IIS server that shares port 80/443 with SSRS.
- Screen-reader output (NVDA, JAWS, Narrator) and real touch devices were not tested.
- 320 px: the Clients table scrolls about 22 px sideways (below the brief's 360 / 390 px acceptance widths).
- Cold-load performance under throttling was not measured; the bundle budget is the size check above.
