## Sequencing and safeguards

- Start with correcting closure wording, verifying Engineer notifications, SLA terminology and controlled client-specific calendar verification. The note-only fixture result is observed; whether configured holiday exclusions work is unverified.
- Use the existing report library before building new dashboards. Team-workload preview succeeded; other report outputs and exports remain untested. Counts have not been independently recalculated from underlying records.
- Do not infer that requests, problems, changes, monitoring or shared knowledge must be added to this product. Establish where those processes belong before commissioning features.
- Assess shared knowledge and continual improvement needs once scope is agreed; identify the authoritative tool and proportionate ownership, review and outcome tracking.
- The labeled non-production UAT case, escalation and commitment are persistent and may affect queues, notifications, reports and dashboard figures. Agree their disposition with the environment owner; deletion or data reset requires approval.

**Coverage limits:** The roadmap does not establish full application coverage, production readiness, a formal ITIL maturity level, or an exhaustive security assessment. Reprioritize when remaining modules, role combinations, SLA boundaries and report outputs have been tested.

**Controlled SLA fixture, 5 November 2026:** The non-production test client UAT_SLA_20261105, its project, [contract CT-1005](https://nb01994.vitronic.de/contracts/5) and [case CS-1298](https://nb01994.vitronic.de/cases/9498) used support hours 09:00–17:00 every day in Asia/Dubai, a 40-support-hour response and resolution target, and customer-wait pause off. The contract notes described fictional 6 November as an excluded holiday. From the displayed clock start of 5 November 10:27, the application displayed 10 November 10:27 for both deadlines. Independent arithmetic gives 10 November 10:27 when 6 November is counted, and 11 November 10:27 when it is excluded. The note-only exclusion was therefore not applied in this fixture. No holiday-date input appeared in the inspected contract form; a free-text note is not a configured holiday. The client edit form showed no holiday/calendar field, and Administration searches for “holiday” and “calendar” each returned zero pages. This confirms a gap in the inspected UI against the user-requested client calendar, not that configuration is impossible through every route; uninspected routes and configured holiday enforcement remain unverified. The client, project and contract remain active and the case remains open; these persistent test records can affect non-production metrics.
